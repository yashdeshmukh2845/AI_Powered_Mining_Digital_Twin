IMPORTANT - NATURE OF THIS DATA

These datasets are ENTIRELY SYNTHETIC. They were produced by the numerical
models in `_generator/` and contain no measurements from any real drillhole,
tenement, or company database. Prospect names, coordinates and grades are
fictional. Any resemblance to a real deposit is coincidental.

Because the data is generated rather than collected, it carries no third
party rights, no confidentiality obligation, and no data-sharing restriction.
That is the entire point: it can be redistributed, bundled into software,
published in tutorials, and used in teaching without a licence review.

These datasets must NOT be used as evidence, analogue, or supporting data in
any public resource or reserve statement (JORC, NI 43-101, SNI, or any other
reporting code). They are for education, software testing, benchmarking and
demonstration only.
